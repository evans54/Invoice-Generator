Help & Tips

🚀 Quick Start

  • • Fill in basic invoice details (dates are required)
  • • Add client information to personalize your invoice
  • • Add services with descriptions, quantities, and rates
  • • Preview updates automatically as you type

Invoice Status

  • Draft: Work in progress
  • Pending: Sent to client
  • Paid: Payment received

Currency Support

Automatic conversion between USD, KSH, TZS, EURO, and GBP. Rates are updated regularly.

Saving & History

Save drafts to continue later. Access invoice history to edit or duplicate previous invoices.

PDF Generation

Professional PDFs with your business branding. Includes payment instructions and terms.

💡 Pro Tips

  • • Use descriptive service names for clarity
  • • Set due dates 14-30 days from issue date
  • • Include payment terms in the notes section
  • • Save drafts frequently to avoid losing work
  • • Duplicate successful invoices as templates
